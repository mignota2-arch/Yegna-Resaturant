Our Story

Revolutionizing the dining experience in Ethiopia.

Why "Yegna"?

Yegna means "Ours" in Amharic. We believe the dining experience belongs to the customer. By removing the wait-time for menus and bills, we put the power of the meal back into your hands.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality Ethiopian and International cuisine with the speed and efficiency of modern technology. No more waving for a waiter—just scan, order, and enjoy.
